# Land verification report template

This is the structure of every Fidero land verification report. Sections appear in this order. Each section lists the evidence that must be attached. The scope wording in section 12 is fixed and must not be changed without the lawyer's and insurer's approval.

Reports are reviewed by two people before release: the lawyer who signed the legal section and one other Fidero reviewer.

## Verdict colours

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green | No material issue found within scope. The buyer may proceed, subject to the conditions listed. |
| Amber | Issues found that can be resolved before purchase. The report lists what must happen and who must do it. Do not pay until resolved. |
| Red | A material issue that cannot be resolved by the buyer, or a risk Fidero will not advise proceeding with. Do not buy. |

A report cannot be green if any single check in sections 4 to 10 is red.

## 1. Cover page

- Report reference number and date.
- Client name and contact.
- Plot description: locality, plot number, site plan reference.
- Verdict: red, amber or green, in large text.
- Validity: the report describes the position on the dates of the searches and the site visit. It is not a guarantee of future events.
- Names and licence numbers of the lawyer and the surveyor who signed.

## 2. Summary

- Verdict and the three to five reasons that drove it.
- Conditions the client must meet before paying, if amber or green.
- Recommended next step.

Keep this to one page. Write for a reader who will read nothing else.

## 3. Scope of engagement

- What the client asked Fidero to check.
- Documents the client supplied, with dates received.
- Dates of each search and the site visit.
- Anything the client asked for that is outside scope, and why.

## 4. Plot identity

**Checks.** The plot the client is being sold is the plot on the site plan, and that plot is the one the surveyor found on the ground.

**Evidence attached.**

- Copy of the site plan supplied by the seller.
- Ghana Post GPS address.
- Surveyor's coordinates of each pillar found.
- Satellite image with the site plan boundary overlaid.
- Photos of each pillar and of the plot from each corner.

**Verdict for this section.** Red if the ground does not match the site plan. Amber if pillars are missing but the boundary is otherwise consistent. Green if ground, plan and coordinates agree.

## 5. Lands Commission searches

**Checks.** Official searches at the Lands Commission on the plot and on the seller's name. Registered interests, caveats, pending applications, previous transfers.

**Evidence attached.**

- Official search result on the plot, with the Lands Commission stamp and date.
- Official search result on the seller's name.
- Receipts for search fees.
- Lawyer's note on what the results mean.

**Verdict for this section.** Red if another person holds a registered interest the seller cannot explain, or a caveat blocks transfer. Amber if the search is pending or the record is incomplete. Green if the record is consistent with the seller's claim or the plot is unregistered customary land with no adverse entry.

## 6. Surveyor's report

**Checks.** The licensed surveyor confirms the plot's position against the cadastral record and neighbouring plots, and notes overlaps.

**Evidence attached.**

- Surveyor's signed report with licence number.
- Cadastral extract or composite plan if available.
- Sketch showing the plot and its neighbours.
- Any overlap measured in metres.

**Verdict for this section.** Red if the plot overlaps a neighbour or a registered plot. Amber if the overlap is small and the surveyor advises it can be corrected. Green if no overlap.

## 7. Overlay checks

**Checks.** Whether the plot falls within a government acquisition, a road reserve, a wetland, a flood zone, a known dispute area, or a zoning that does not allow the client's intended use.

**Evidence attached.**

- Overlay map for each layer checked, with source and date.
- Planning scheme extract or local assembly confirmation where available.
- Flood and wetland indicators from satellite imagery and site observation.
- Note on any known dispute in the area, with source.

**Verdict for this section.** Red if the plot is inside an acquisition, road reserve or protected wetland. Amber if near a boundary of one, or if zoning is uncertain. Green if clear.

## 8. Seller authority and consent

**Checks.** Who the seller is, what interest they hold, and whether the people who must consent have consented.

**Evidence attached.**

- Seller's identity document.
- Document showing the seller's interest: allocation note, indenture, lease, or family resolution.
- Written confirmation from the stool, skin or family head that the seller may sell this plot.
- Signed consent form from the required parties.
- Video recording of the consent meeting: date, place, names, the plot described, the consent spoken aloud. Lawyer present. Witnesses named.
- Lawyer's note on who else may need to consent.

**Verdict for this section.** Red if the seller cannot show an interest, or the authority denies the sale. Amber if consent is promised but not yet recorded. Green if the interest is shown and consent is signed and recorded.

## 9. Chain of ownership

**Checks.** How the seller came to hold the plot, and whether the chain has gaps.

**Evidence attached.**

- Copies of previous transfers, indentures and receipts.
- Lawyer's summary of the chain, with dates.
- Any inconsistency noted.

**Verdict for this section.** Red if a previous transfer shows the plot was sold to someone else and not recovered. Amber if documents are missing but the authority confirms the chain. Green if the chain is complete.

## 10. Occupation and ground conditions

**Checks.** Who is on the land now, any structures, any sign of dispute or land guard activity, access, and physical conditions that affect building.

**Evidence attached.**

- Site visit notes with date and names of visitors.
- Photos of any structure, fence or marking.
- Notes of conversations with neighbours, without personal data beyond what is needed.
- Access route and road condition.

**Verdict for this section.** Red if the plot is occupied by someone claiming ownership, or there was any threat to the team. Amber if there are minor encroachments. Green if vacant and undisputed.

## 11. Verdict and conditions

- Overall verdict with the section verdicts in a table.
- For amber: the exact steps to reach green, who does each, and what evidence closes it.
- For green: the conditions the purchase agreement and escrow should include, such as consent documents delivered before release.
- Recommended next steps: escrow, conveyancing, title registration.

## 12. Scope and limitation statement

This wording is fixed. Attach it to every report in full.

> This report sets out the results of the checks listed in section 3, carried out on the dates stated, using the documents the client supplied and the public and official records available to Fidero on those dates. It describes the position as found on those dates only.
>
> The report does not guarantee title. Title to land in Ghana can only be confirmed by registration at the Lands Commission, and registered title can be challenged. The report does not replace legal advice on the purchase agreement, which the client should obtain from the lawyer named in this report or another lawyer of their choice.
>
> The report relies on records held by third parties, including the Lands Commission, traditional authorities and local assemblies. Fidero has not verified the completeness of those records and cannot do so. The report relies on statements made by the seller, the traditional authority and witnesses, which Fidero has recorded but cannot independently prove.
>
> The report covers only the plot described on the cover page and only the matters listed in sections 4 to 10. Anything not listed is outside scope. Fidero's liability for this report is limited to the fee paid for it, except where the law does not allow that limit. Fidero holds professional indemnity insurance in respect of this report [policy reference TO VERIFY].
>
> The report is for the named client only and may not be relied on by any other person without Fidero's written agreement.

## 13. Signatures

- Lawyer: name, licence number, signature, date.
- Surveyor: name, licence number, signature, date.
- Fidero reviewer: name, signature, date.

## 14. Appendices

- A. All documents supplied by the client.
- B. All search results and receipts.
- C. Surveyor's full report and coordinates.
- D. Overlay maps.
- E. Consent forms and the video recording reference.
- F. Site visit photos with timestamp and GPS tag.
- G. Data handling note: how the client and seller data in this report are stored and for how long, under the Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843).
