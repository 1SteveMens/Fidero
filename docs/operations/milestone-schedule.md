# Construction milestone schedule

This is the standard schedule for a Fidero-managed build. Payments are released from the client's held funds only when a milestone is confirmed. Confirmation needs two things: evidence from the contractor through the milestone photo app, and sign-off by an independent inspector.

Percentages are assumptions for a standard single-storey house. The quantity surveyor sets the actual split for each project from the bill of quantities.

## How a payment is released

1. Contractor reports the milestone complete in the app.
2. Contractor uploads the required photos and video. Each is geotagged and time-stamped by the app. Photos must be taken on site and cannot be uploaded from the gallery.
3. Fidero checks the evidence against the checklist below.
4. The independent inspector visits within 5 working days (assumption) and completes the checklist on site.
5. The inspector submits a pass, a pass with defects, or a fail.
6. On a pass, Fidero instructs release of the milestone payment to the contractor. On a pass with defects, release is held back by the defect value until fixed. On a fail, no release. The contractor fixes and resubmits.
7. The client sees the evidence, the inspector's result and the payment on the dashboard.

The client can ask for an extra inspection at any time at $50-100 (assumption).

## Milestone 1: Foundation

**Evidence from contractor.**

- Photos of the cleared site and the setting out, with pegs and lines visible.
- Photos of excavated trenches showing depth, with a tape measure in frame.
- Photos of the reinforcement in place before concrete, from at least two angles.
- Video walking the full trench line before and after concrete.
- Photos of the cured foundation and the first courses of the footing wall.
- Delivery notes for cement, aggregate and reinforcement.

**Inspector checklist.**

- Setting out matches the approved drawing and the plot boundary from the verification report.
- Trench depth and width match the drawing or the engineer's instruction.
- Soil condition noted. Any water or soft ground reported.
- Reinforcement size, spacing and cover match the drawing.
- Concrete mix and curing appear adequate. Note any honeycombing.
- Damp-proof course present where specified.
- Footing wall level and plumb.
- Hardcore and blinding complete under the floor slab if specified.
- Materials on site match delivery notes.
- Safety: trenches not left open and unmarked.

## Milestone 2: Blocks

**Evidence from contractor.**

- Photos of each elevation at window sill level and at lintel level.
- Photos of block bonding and joint thickness close up.
- Photos of door and window openings with a tape measure in frame.
- Video walking the inside and outside of the building.
- Delivery notes for blocks, cement and sand.

**Inspector checklist.**

- Wall positions match the drawing. Room dimensions checked at two points.
- Walls plumb and level. Check with a spirit level at each corner.
- Block quality: consistent size, no crumbling. Note the source.
- Mortar joints consistent and fully filled.
- Opening sizes match the drawing.
- Reinforcement or columns in place where the drawing requires.
- Conduit for electrics placed where specified.
- No cracks in the foundation or footing wall since milestone 1.

## Milestone 3: Lintel

**Evidence from contractor.**

- Photos of lintel formwork and reinforcement before concrete, at each opening.
- Photos of the cast lintel beam after striking the formwork.
- Video around the full lintel line.
- Photos of block work above the lintel to wall plate level.
- Delivery notes for reinforcement and cement.

**Inspector checklist.**

- Lintel over every opening, with bearing on each side as the drawing requires.
- Reinforcement size and position as specified.
- Concrete cured for the required time before load.
- No sagging, cracks or honeycombing in the beam.
- Ring beam present if the drawing requires one.
- Block work above lintel plumb and level.
- Wall plate level and ready for the roof.

## Milestone 4: Roof

**Evidence from contractor.**

- Photos of roof timber or steel structure before covering, showing sizes and spacing.
- Photos of anchoring to the wall plate.
- Photos of the roof covering from the ground on each side.
- Video from inside the roof space.
- Photos of fascia, gutters and downpipes if specified.
- Delivery notes for timber or steel, roofing sheets and fixings.

**Inspector checklist.**

- Structure member sizes and spacing match the drawing.
- Timber treated or steel coated as specified.
- Structure anchored to the wall plate against wind uplift.
- Roof covering laid with correct overlap and fixings. No visible gaps.
- Ridge, hips and valleys sealed.
- Gutters fall toward downpipes.
- No sign of leaks after the first rain, if one has occurred.
- Ceiling joists in place if specified.

## Milestone 5: Finishing

**Evidence from contractor.**

- Photos of each room showing plaster, floor, ceiling, doors and windows.
- Photos of the electrical distribution board, sockets and switches.
- Photos of plumbing fittings, water tank and septic tank or connection.
- Video walking through every room with lights and taps tested.
- Photos of external render and paint on each elevation.
- Delivery notes and receipts for fittings.

**Inspector checklist.**

- Plaster even, no hollow spots when tapped, no cracks.
- Floors level and finished as specified.
- Doors and windows fitted, open and close, locks work.
- Electrics: all circuits tested, earth present, board labelled. Electrician's certificate if available `[TO VERIFY]`.
- Plumbing: water runs at every outlet, no leaks, drains clear, septic or sewer connection complete.
- Paint coverage complete, colours as specified.
- External works as specified: drainage, paths, boundary wall if in scope.
- Site cleared of debris.
- Defects list prepared with photos. Each defect valued.

## Milestone 6: Handover and retention

- Keys and all certificates handed over.
- Client or their representative walks the house with the inspector, in person or on video.
- Retention held for the defects period `[TO VERIFY]` and released when the inspector confirms the defects list is closed.

## Rules that apply to every milestone

- Evidence must come through the app with the app's own geotag and timestamp. Evidence from any other source is not accepted.
- The inspector must not be chosen or paid by the contractor.
- A contractor who submits evidence for work not done is removed from the panel.
- Each milestone's cost is re-priced in cedis against the dollar guide before release. Any change is shown to the client before they approve.
- The client can view all evidence at any time. The client cannot release a payment without the inspector's pass.
- Every variation to scope is agreed in writing in the app before the work is done.
