# Survey: what else do you pay for at home?

One question for every lead and every webinar attendee (`docs/validation-plan.md` section 4b). It tells us which line to test after land. It promises nothing and takes no money.

## The question

> Apart from land, what else do you pay for at home? Tick all that apply.

| Option, as the client sees it | Tracker column | Candidate line it points to |
|---|---|---|
| Light, water, rent or TV bills | Also pays: Household bills | Household bills paid directly (new) |
| School fees | Also pays: School fees | Payments to institutions (planned) |
| Hospital bills or medicines | Also pays: Hospital or medicines | Payments to institutions; repeat prescriptions (new) |
| Costs I share with brothers and sisters | Also pays: Shared family costs | Family pot (new) |
| Funerals | Also pays: Funerals | Funeral package |
| Building a house bit by bit | Also pays: Building bit by bit | Construction (planned) |
| Someone to watch my land or house | Also pays: Watching land or a house | Home watch (new) |
| Passports, Ghana Card and other papers | Also pays: Paperwork | Paperwork and errands (planned) |
| Getting ready for a trip home | Also pays: Trip home | Trip home bundle (new) |
| Church, mosque or hometown dues | Also pays: Church or hometown dues | Payments to institutions; project funds |

## Where to ask it

- **Landing page.** Optional tick boxes on the order form. The answers appear in the request message as "Also pays at home: …".
- **Webinars.** The last slide, as a poll or a show of hands. Write down each attendee's answers against their name if they leave contact details.
- **WhatsApp.** After a lead's first reply, send: "One quick question to help us plan: apart from land, what else do you pay for at home? Bills, school fees, hospital, costs shared with family, funerals, building, someone to watch your land, papers, a trip home, or church or hometown dues?"

## Recording and reading it

1. Tick "Yes" in the matching "Also pays" columns of the lead's row in the tracker. Leave a column blank if they did not mention it.
2. The Review tab shows how many leads answered and the share who need each one.
3. After 50 answers (assumption), take the top one or two needs to the day-90 review.

Do not offer or sell any of these lines because someone ticked a box. Each line opens only after its gate.
