# Decision: launch with two front doors, family payments and property checks

Date: 12 November 2026
Decided by: the founder

## Context

Land verification was the launch product. A person abroad buys land or a house once or twice in a lifetime, so a business built only on land checks gets few transactions per client and must keep finding new buyers. Most money sent home goes on everyday family spending, school fees and health, and on housing. Many people build a house at home over several years. The research is older and mixed; none of it measures how often people abroad buy land.

## Decision

1. **Two front doors from the start.**
   - **Family payments against proof:** school fees, hospital bills and household bills. These are frequent, they build the habit and they bring users.
   - **Property checks on land and houses:** the largest sums, the highest fees and the strongest trust story.
2. **Test building in stages alongside.** A few clients building a house pay their builder by stage, each stage inspected and photographed first. Building is both frequent and large, and may become the strongest single line.
3. **Measure users and repeat use**, not only reports. The day-90 review counts active clients, repeat clients and transactions as well as reports.
4. **Escrow, verified listings and the other lines keep their gates.** Land stays the main use of escrow when it opens.

## How family payments work before a licensed partner is in place

Tenmina never holds client money. Until a licensed partner can pay on a client's behalf, the model is **verify and confirm**:

1. Tenmina verifies the school, hospital or provider: registration, a bank or mobile money account in its own name, and a named contact who confirms the invoice.
2. The client pays that verified account directly, from their own bank or money transfer service.
3. The institution confirms receipt, and Tenmina sends the client the official receipt.

Tenmina charges a fee per payment or a monthly plan `[TO VERIFY]` through its payment provider. Building stages work the same way: Tenmina inspects and confirms the stage and the builder's invoice, and the client pays the builder's verified account.

## Why not drop land

Land and houses are where the dramatic losses are, which makes them the best marketing story. They earn the largest fee per client and are what bank escrow is designed for.

## Watch-outs

- Everyday payments are crowded: money transfer apps already send money home cheaply. Tenmina wins on verification, direct payment to the institution and proof, not on price. Fees per payment are small, so this door works only at volume.
- Paying on a client's behalf needs a licensed partner first, the same rule as escrow.

## Consequences

- Validation plan: goal, two front doors, the verify-and-confirm model, house checks, the building test and new day-90 criteria.
- Tracker: Clients and Build stages tabs, more needs and institution types, and Review counts of active and repeat clients.
- Business plan, proposal, README and CLAUDE.md: the launch is described as two front doors, not land alone.
- Specs: document 01 purpose and change history; document 04 R0 enablers.

All numbers are working assumptions to test, not verified data.
