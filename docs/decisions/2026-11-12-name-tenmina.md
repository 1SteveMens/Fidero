# Decision: the company and product name is Tenmina

Date: 12 November 2026. Decided by the founder. Replaces the working name Fidero.

## Why Tenmina

From the Parable of the Minas (Luke 19:11-27). A nobleman entrusts a mina to each of his servants. The faithful servant reports: "your mina has made ten minas" (Luke 19:16), and is told "Well done, good servant; because you were faithful in a very little" (Luke 19:17). That is the job: handle what someone abroad entrusts, faithfully, and show the proof.

Possible line: "Faithful in the little things."

## Names considered and dropped

Each was screened with a web search, not a trademark search.

| Name | Why dropped |
|---|---|
| Surely | An existing drinks brand |
| Hearth, Settle | Payments and financing fintechs already use them |
| Faraja | Safaricom's buy-now-pay-later product in Kenya |
| Tulia | Too close to Tulix, a diaspora payments app in Kenya |
| Konfi, Quonfy | Close to Konfío, a Mexican fintech, and other "Konf" payment names |
| Calmora, Certivo, Serenta, Minara | Taken, or too close to a finance app |
| Mina, Minas, Myna | Mina Money is a UK money-transfer app; "minas" also means mines; "myna" is a bird |
| Local-language names (Ahoto, Gyidie, Aminci and others) | The founder chose an English-language name for all markets |

Minavo is the reserve name if Tenmina fails the trademark search.

## What changed

- Every document, both apps, the landing page, the prototype, the design canvases and the business plan use Tenmina.
- App identifiers: `com.tenmina.client` (Tenmina) and `com.tenmina.field` (Tenmina Field). Nothing was published under the old ones.
- Reference prefixes: case and escrow references `FID-` became `TMN-`; deposit and job references `FDR-` became `TMR-`.
- Code packages: `@fidero/*` became `@tenmina/*`.
