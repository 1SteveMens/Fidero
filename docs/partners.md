# Partners and first asks

Fidero depends on partners for money handling, legal work, surveying, building, distribution and insurance. This page lists each partner type, why Fidero needs them, what they get, and the first ask.

All figures are assumptions. Unknown numbers are `[TO VERIFY]`.

## 1. Escrow partner bank or licensed escrow agent

**Why.** Bank of Ghana guidance means escrow must run through a licensed bank or a licensed escrow agent. Fidero never holds client money itself.

**What they get.** Hard-currency inflows from diaspora clients, new accounts, a share of the escrow fee (1-2% of price, split `[TO VERIFY]`), and a pipeline of verified land for mortgage lending.

**First ask.** A meeting with the head of diaspora banking or retail products. Bring the report numbers from the 90-day validation. Ask for a pilot of 10 escrows under a simple agreement: client deposits, Fidero confirms conditions met, bank releases to seller.

**Open questions.** Fee split. Who signs the release instruction. Whether the bank can take deposits from abroad without the client opening a Ghana account `[TO VERIFY]`. Timeline for a status API or a daily statement.

**Targets.** Two banks and one licensed escrow agent in parallel `[TO VERIFY]`.

## 2. Law firms

**Why.** Lands Commission searches, document review, consent meetings, conveyancing and title registration need a lawyer. The legal section of every report is signed by a lawyer.

**What they get.** Steady flow of search and conveyancing work from diaspora clients who would not otherwise find them. Conveyancing revenue at $300-800 per transaction (assumption).

**First ask.** A per-report fee for searches and review `[TO VERIFY]`, a 10-working-day turnaround for the legal section, and a named lawyer who attends consent meetings. Later, a retainer.

**Terms to agree.** Scope of their sign-off. Confidentiality. Data handling under Act 843. Their own professional indemnity cover.

## 3. Licensed surveyors

**Why.** The surveyor check is the ground truth of the report. Only a licensed surveyor can sign the surveyor's section.

**What they get.** Regular site jobs with travel paid, and later cadastral work for the traditional authority records programme.

**First ask.** A per-visit fee `[TO VERIFY]` plus travel, a 5-working-day turnaround from visit to signed section, and GPS coordinates plus photos delivered in a standard format.

**Terms to agree.** Licence check with the Licensed Surveyors Association of Ghana `[TO VERIFY]`. Safety rule: no visit to a plot flagged as disputed without a risk screen and a second person.

## 4. Contractors

**Why.** Construction management needs builders who accept milestone payments and inspection.

**What they get.** Diaspora clients with a budget held safely, paid promptly at each milestone, and materials delivered to site. No chasing clients abroad for money.

**First ask.** Join a vetted panel. Provide company registration, past projects with references, a site the inspector can visit, and acceptance of the milestone schedule in `docs/operations/milestone-schedule.md`.

**Terms to agree.** Fixed-price or bill-of-quantities contracts. Payment only on inspector sign-off. Defects period. Removal from the panel for any diverted payment.

**Also needed.** A quantity surveyor to produce bills of quantities and an independent inspector per region, both contracted per job `[TO VERIFY]`.

## 5. Diaspora associations, churches and hometown associations

**Why.** They are where the first clients are. Members trust a recommendation from the association more than an advert.

**What they get.** A free talk or webinar on land fraud for members, a member discount `[TO VERIFY]`, and a referral reward paid to the association or the member.

**First ask.** A 30-minute slot at a meeting or a webinar for members, and a named contact who will share the offer.

**Targets.** Ten associations in the first 90 days across the UK, US, Canada, Germany and the Netherlands.

## 6. Remittance companies

**Why.** They already serve the same customers and see the money flow for land and building.

**What they get.** A product to offer customers who send money for land or construction, and a share of verification or escrow fees `[TO VERIFY]`. Later, escrow deposits routed through their rails.

**First ask.** A co-marketing pilot: they tell customers sending money to Ghana for land about Fidero, Fidero tracks referrals, both sides review after 90 days. No integration needed at first.

**Targets.** Two companies `[TO VERIFY]`.

## 7. Professional indemnity insurer

**Why.** A wrong verification is the biggest risk to the business. Cover protects the client, the professionals and Fidero.

**What they get.** A new line of business with a clear scope definition and a documented process.

**First ask.** A quote for professional indemnity cover on the verification service, with the report scope wording from `docs/operations/verification-report-template.md` attached. Ask what process changes would lower the premium.

**Open questions.** Cover limit per report and in aggregate `[TO VERIFY]`. Whether the lawyer's and surveyor's own cover counts. Whether cover extends to construction inspection.

## 8. Partners for the product lines beyond land

- **Structural engineers and building inspectors.** Inspect existing buildings before purchase. First ask: a per-inspection fee [TO VERIFY] and a standard inspection report. Phase 2.
- **Developers.** Sell off-plan homes with stage payments held in escrow, and list verified units. First ask: a pilot estate with escrowed stage payments. Phase 3.
- **Suppliers.** Paid directly for project funds and construction materials. First ask: accept direct payment against invoices and deliver to site with proof of delivery. Phase 3.

## 9. Later partners

- **Traditional authorities.** Stools, skins and families whose allocation records Fidero digitises under a revenue share, and who later list verified plots. Approach after the verification service has a track record in their area.
- **Lands Commission.** Formal access to searches or data `[TO VERIFY]`. Approach once volumes justify it.
- **Materials suppliers.** Bulk supply at a margin of 3-7% (assumption). Approach in phase 3.
- **Mortgage lenders and developers.** Collateral checks and clean-title land. Approach with the first bank contract in phase 3.
